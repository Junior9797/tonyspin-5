Start in four steps

  1. Set a stake. Type in the amount you want to risk for the round; the multiplier is applied to this base bet.

  3. Reveal tiles. Tap any square — a gem lifts your payout, while a mine ends that round straight away.
  4. Cash out in time. Lock in the current multiplier whenever you are happy; on this game, restraint matters more than luck.

Frequently asked questions

1. Can the game move a mine after I start picking?

No. On a properly audited site, the layout is fixed when the round begins, and provably fair tools let you confirm the result afterwards.

It is based on the real odds of your exact sequence of safe picks, with a small house edge included — more mines and more picks both push it higher.

3. Is there a best number of mines?

No mine count changes the expected return. Fewer mines make the ride smoother; more mines trade hit rate for larger potential payouts. The right choice depends on your temperament.
